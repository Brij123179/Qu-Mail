🧪 Physical Entropy vs Quantum Entropy Explainer

1. Cloudflare's Lava Lamps (Entropy Generation)

Cloudflare points cameras at 100 lava lamps in its lobby to capture physical chaos (wax blob movement & pixel noise) to seed cryptographic keys. This solves "How do we generate true random keys?" but cannot detect eavesdroppers in transit.

2. Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) & Physics

QKD uses single photons over optical fiber/satellites (BB84 protocol). Under the Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle and No-Cloning Theorem (|ψ⟩), any measurement by an eavesdropper alters photon states.

The Soap Bubble Analogy: A quantum key is like a key made of soap bubbles — the instant an eavesdropper touches it to copy it, it pops, and both parties detect the intrusion!

3. QuMail ETSI GS QKD 014 Key Manager Integration

QuMail software interfaces with Key Managers via the industry-standard ETSI GS QKD 014 REST API. Keys are generated, issued once, and burned immediately upon recipient decryption (returning HTTP 410 Gone on key reuse attempts).
